# Croods illustration artwork

Artwork by Vijay Verma (realvjy), from [illlustrations.co](https://illlustrations.co/) and the [official avatars repository](https://github.com/realvjy/illlustrations-avatars).

Downloaded: 2026-10-20. Revision: 22612edc6cba5bce9296a15bc892d83e248eea27.

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See ARTWORK-LICENSE.md.

Adaptations: bundled source SVG paths are composed into a joint-driven model. Known white skin and red hair and yellow clothing fills are replaced with local color variables. The head/face source offsets are retained, then fitted to identity proportions. Profile/back contours and the articulated body, hands, shoes, outlines and shading are local adaptations; they are simplified rather than exact wardrobe or portrait renderings. Upper-body artwork is also shown separately as labeled style references.
